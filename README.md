## Pricing Models Based on Access Points, Users, and Locations

Managed wireless pricing can be structured around different usage metrics depending on business needs.

### Managed Wireless Pricing Per Access Point

This model calculates cost based on the number of deployed access points. **Managed wireless pricing per access point** is ideal for environments where coverage density and performance are critical, such as warehouses, campuses, and large offices.

### Managed Wireless Pricing Per User

In user-centric environments, **managed wireless pricing per user** aligns costs directly with workforce size. This model works well for organizations with predictable headcounts and usage patterns.

### Managed Wireless Pricing Per Site and Location

For multi-branch enterprises, **managed wireless pricing per site** and **managed wireless pricing per location** offer clarity and scalability. Each location is priced based on size, complexity, and service scope, making expansion easier to plan and budget.
